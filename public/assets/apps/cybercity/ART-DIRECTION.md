# 赛博城市原创美术

Created 2026-09-08 using the built-in imagegen tool (not the CLI). Generated specifically for this project. These are original environment/advertising assets inspired by the genre and visual density of Cyberpunk 2077; they are not extracted game models. Generated source images are preserved in the Codex generated_images directory; the project uses copies here.

## Runtime usage

- `facade-atlas.png`: four quadrants, accessed by UV coordinates on physical 3D facade/storefront panels. Top left: residential; top right: industrial; bottom left: ramen shop; bottom right: cybernetics shop.
- `distant-skyline.png`: distant matte painting on four surrounding planes; not navigable geometry.
- `neural-poster.png`: original in-world advertising artwork on 3D billboard meshes.

Used by the independent Cyber City experience at `/?app=cybercity`. Two enterable rooms (夜食 and NEURAL) use real room geometry, furnishings, interactive lighting and seating.

Near buildings, shop roofs, sidewalks, bridges, poles, vehicles, terminals and cables are real Three.js geometry. Movement and picking operate on the three-dimensional scene. These textures do not replace a fully modeled AAA city or physically simulated inhabitants.
